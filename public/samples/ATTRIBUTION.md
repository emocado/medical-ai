# Sample files: attribution and notice

## Fictional demo documents

The lab reports and pharmacy labels in this folder are **fictional demo data** made for testing HealthMate:

- `report-2026-03-checkup.png` / `.pdf`
- `report-2026-09-followup.png` / `.pdf`
- `report-2026-09-urgent.png`
- `pills-daily-regimen.png`
- `pill-clarithromycin.png`

The patient (Lim Siew Lan), clinics (Klinik Sejahtera Demo, Klinik Keluarga Demo), laboratory (MediLab Demo Sdn Bhd), pharmacy (Farmasi Sejahtera Demo), doctors and pathologist ("Dr. A. Rahman (demo)" and others) are all invented. Any resemblance to real people or organisations is coincidental. Every document carries a "SAMPLE — FOR DEMO ONLY" watermark. They are not medical records and must not be used for clinical decisions.

The sources are in `scripts/samples/src/`. To rebuild the files, run `node scripts/samples/build.mjs`.
